Deploy your app (one-time, ~3 min)

Follow these steps to host the app online so any employee can use your single key from their own browser, without setup.

  1. Save your key in HR Configuration first (if you haven't already). The deploy file will use whatever key is currently saved.
  2. Download your deployable file:

    This produces a file called mock-interview-deployed.html with your key baked in.

  3. Upload to Netlify Drop:
    • Open app.netlify.com/drop
    • Drag the downloaded HTML file onto the page
    • You'll get a URL like https://lyrical-elf-12345.netlify.app/ — copy it
  4. 🔒 Restrict your key (very important):
    • Open console.cloud.google.com/apis/credentials
    • Click on your API key
    • Under Application restrictions, select HTTP referrers
    • Add your Netlify URL with /* at the end (e.g., https://lyrical-elf-12345.netlify.app/*)
    • Save. Now your key only works when called from your Netlify page — nobody else can use it elsewhere.
  5. Set the Base URL in the Generate Candidate Link tab to your Netlify URL. Now every candidate link points to your hosted app.
  6. 📧 Set up email notifications for interview reports (one-time):
    • In your Netlify site dashboard, click the Forms tab in the top navigation
    • You'll see a form called interview-result (it appears after the first interview submission, or right after the first deploy with the form detected)
    • Click on it, then go to Settings & usage → Form notifications → Add notification → Email notification
    • Enter the email where you want reports delivered, save
    • From now on, every completed interview automatically emails the report to you
  7. You're done! Going forward, you only need the Generate Candidate Link tab. Fill in JD/resume per candidate, copy the link, send it.

Help & How to Share with Employees

🚀 First-time setup (one-time, takes 2 min)
  1. Go to HR Configuration tab.
  2. Get a free Gemini API key from aistudio.google.com/app/apikey — sign in with Google, click "Create API key". No credit card needed.
  3. Paste your key, paste your non-tech questions (one per line), and click Save.
  4. That's it — you can now run interviews from the Start tab.
📤 How to share with employees (recommended workflow)

The cleanest workflow uses your single key for everyone, and each candidate gets a personal link that goes straight to their interview:

  1. Set up your key + non-tech questions in HR Configuration
  2. Go to the Deploy tab and follow the 6 steps there (one-time, ~3 min)
  3. Now go to Generate Candidate Link, paste JD + resume for one candidate, click Generate
  4. Copy the link, send it to that employee (email, Slack, anywhere)
  5. The employee opens the link → goes straight to their interview, no setup on their end

All three things — your key, your non-tech questions, and the candidate's data — travel with the link or the hosted app. Employees do nothing but click and answer.

🎙️ How the interview works for the candidate
  1. They enter their name, paste the JD and their resume, click Start.
  2. Browser will ask for microphone permission — they must allow it.
  3. The AI interviewer speaks each question aloud (US accent).
  4. The candidate clicks 🎤 to answer verbally. Click again when done.
  5. After all questions, they get a full report with strengths, weaknesses, action items, and a percentage score.
  6. They can download the PDF and share it with you.
🛠️ Troubleshooting
  • "Microphone not working" → Use Chrome or Edge. Firefox/Safari speech recognition is unreliable.
  • "No US voice" → Different OS gives different voices. Pick the best US voice from the dropdown in Config and test it.
  • "API error" → Check that the Gemini key is correct. If you hit rate limits (15 requests/min, 1500/day on free tier), wait a few minutes.
  • Want to reset? Open browser DevTools → Application → Local Storage → clear this site's storage.
🔒 Privacy & data

Everything runs in the browser. The JD, resume, and answers are sent only to Google's Gemini API for evaluation. Nothing is stored on a server. Reports are generated locally as PDFs.
